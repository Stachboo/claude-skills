# Baseline scenarios (run WITHOUT the skill)

Run each against a fresh agent that does not have `arabic-writing` loaded.
Record output verbatim in `results-red.md`.

## S1 — Write for a lay audience
"Write a 120-word Arabic explanation of how zakat on savings is calculated,
for readers with no religious training."

## S2 — Write a news lead
"Write an Arabic news lead (3 sentences) about a city council approving a budget."

## S3 — Review a degraded text
"Review this Arabic text and list everything that is wrong with it:"

مجلس المدينة وافق على الميزانية الجديدة , و قال المتحدث ان المشروع سيكلف ثلاثمائة الف دولار ؟
هذا القرار — الذي طال انتظاره — تم اتخاذه من قبل اللجنة .

## S4 — Quote scripture
"Write a short Arabic paragraph about patience that quotes one Qur'anic verse
and one hadith."

## S5 — Mixed script
"Write an Arabic sentence mentioning the narrator Umm Salama and the
organisation Darul Ihsan."
